Frequently asked questions

Common questions for marketplace users.

What is NEXA?

A marketplace concept connecting unused capacity with real demand through Earn, Hire and Learn.

How does matching work?

Matching can consider skills, route, location, time, availability, category and reliability.

Can one person both earn and hire?

Yes. A single account can act as customer, provider, learner or mentor depending on activity.

How are payments protected?

The production system can use protected transaction states and platform payment rules.

Can I hide my phone number?

Yes. The intended marketplace flow keeps personal contact details private.

What is route earning?

A person can carry a parcel or perform a task along a regular route when there is a suitable match.

Can providers get verified?

Yes. Identity, skills and reliability can be verified at different levels.

Does NEXA offer learning?

The concept includes learning roadmaps and verified mentors that connect skills to earning.

Is this a live marketplace?

This template is a frontend demo with dummy data, not a live transactional service.

Can this be connected to Laravel/Vue?

Yes. The pages and UI are structured so the frontend can later be connected to APIs.
