Frequently Asked Questions

What is NEXA?

A marketplace connecting unused capacity with real demand through Earn, Hire and Learn.

Can one person both earn and hire?

Yes. A single account can act as customer, provider, learner or mentor depending on activity.

How does smart matching work?

The concept considers skills, route, location, time, availability, category and reliability.

How does route earning work?

A person can carry parcels or perform a task along a regular route when there is a useful match.

Is contact information private?

The intended flow keeps personal contact details hidden and uses platform chat.

Does NEXA include learning?

Yes — learning roadmaps and verified mentors connect practice to earning opportunities.
